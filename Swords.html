The Sword I Want

What to look for in a Sword. Ps I want a Real sword.

High-Carbon Steel: Look for Carbon Steel Grades like 1060, 1095, T10, or 9260 spring steel, which hold an edge and absorb impact without snapping. Avoid stainless steel, as it is brittle and can shatter under stress.

Full Tang (Nakago): The metal of the blade must extend fully through the handle (tsuka). It is secured by bamboo pins (mekugi). Never buy a "rat-tail" tang welded piece for functional use.

Proper Tempering: A functional blade requires a balanced hardness level—typically measured between HRC 45–50 for European styles or differentially hardened edges up to HRC 60 for Japanese swords—to absorb impact without snapping or permanently bending.
